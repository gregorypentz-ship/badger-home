# Dash Badge

Dash Badge is a small obstacle-jumping game with three selectable levels and a customizable player icon.

## How to play

On the title screen, use UP/DOWN to pick a level, LEFT/RIGHT to change the player color, and BACK to change its shape. Press SELECT to start. During a level, press SELECT to jump and BACK to return to the title screen. If you hit an obstacle, press SELECT to retry.

The selected player color and shape are saved. No extra hardware, Wi-Fi, or secrets are required.
